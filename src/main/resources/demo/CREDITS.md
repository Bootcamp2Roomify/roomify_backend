# Demo-library photos

Shown as the "visual concept" only in demo mode (`AI_DEMO_MODE=true`, or when a live AI call fails and
`AI_DEMO_FALLBACK=true`). They are stock photos of real rooms, not edits of the user's photo, and the app
labels them as demo results. All four are CC0 (public domain dedication) via Wikimedia Commons, originally
from Unsplash; resized to 1024 px wide.
